PS1-Style Great White Shark

An authentic, low-poly 3D great white shark asset inspired by retro console limitations. Suitable for aquatic environments, retro games, and low-poly prototypes.

Package Scope

  • 1 Unique Mesh (Rigged skeleton)
  • 1 Texture (128x128px)
  • 2 Animations

What is Inside

  • Great White Shark Model: Fully rigged model developed from a primitive cube.
  • Animations: Pre-configured "Swimming" and "Jump" action sequences.
  • Retro Texture: Authentic pixelated texture map optimized for unfiltered rendering.

Technical Details

  • Geometry: Highly optimized low-poly mesh containing 153 vertices.
